# Content Strategy

`yaya-content-studio` is built around a simple idea: personal content works better when it keeps both lived experience and practical usefulness.

## Free articles

Free articles are for resonance, trust, and discovery.

Good free articles often include:

- A real situation
- A specific reader
- A problem the reader recognizes
- A small but useful takeaway
- Enough personal detail to feel human

Free articles do not need to solve everything. They should help readers feel seen and give them a reason to follow future work.

## Paid articles

Paid articles should solve a concrete problem.

Good paid articles often include:

- The full process
- What failed before the current approach
- Templates or reusable checklists
- Real examples
- Clear boundaries about what the article can and cannot help with

Paid content should not rely on exaggerated success language. Avoid vague promises and phrases like "anyone can do this" when the real situation needs context, effort, or tradeoffs.

## Platform notes

### note

`note` works well for personal context, lived experience, behind-the-scenes thinking, and paid articles that include practical templates.

Useful angles:

- What happened
- Why it mattered
- What did not work
- What changed
- What readers can try

### Medium

Medium works well for structured essays, practical frameworks, and articles that can reach readers outside the creator's immediate community.

Useful angles:

- The problem
- The context
- The method
- The example
- The takeaway

### Threads and X

Threads and X are useful for early signals.

Short posts can test:

- Topic demand
- Wording
- Reader questions
- Whether a personal story has resonance

If a short post gets strong likes or replies, it may be worth turning into a longer note or Medium article.

## A simple weekly review

Once a week:

1. Run `python main.py list`
2. Run `python main.py analyze`
3. Look for categories with repeated engagement
4. Look for articles with likes but no sales
5. Pick one free article and one concrete paid article idea

The goal is not to optimize every number. The goal is to notice patterns before they disappear.
